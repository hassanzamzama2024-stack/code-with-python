## Introduction to TikTok and Account Creation:
How can we cerat Tik Tok account:
- Download the TikTok app from your phone app store.
- Open the TikTok application.
- Click Sign Up.
- Choose a sign-up method such as email, phone number, or another available account.
- Enter your date of birth.
- Create a username and password.
- Add a profile picture.
- Write a short bio describing yourself or your business.
- Your TikTok account is now ready.


##  Account Settings & Privacy and Switching Personal to Business Account on TikTok:
  # How can we switch persnol to Business account on Tik Tok:
- Open TikTok.
- Go to Profile.
- Tap the Menu.
- Select Settings and privacy.
- Tap Account.
- Select the option to Switch to Business Account.
- Choose a category for your business.
- Follow the instructions to complete the setup.
