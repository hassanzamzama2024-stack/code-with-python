# How to Create a TikTok Account:

 - Download the TikTok app from your device's official app store.
- Open TikTok and select Sign up.
- Choose a registration method, such as email or phone number.
- Enter your date of birth when requested.
- Create a username and password.
- Add a profile picture if you want.
- Complete your profile.



## Account Settings & Privacy and Switching Personal to Business  Account on TikTok: 
  TikTok's Account Settings & Privacy section lets you control how your account works and who can interact with you.


 ## Switching from Personal to Business Account:
- Open TikTok.
- Go to Profile.
- Tap the ☰ Menu.
- Select Settings and privacy.
- Go to Account.
- Look for the option to Switch to Business Account.
- Follow the instructions and select an appropriate business category.
